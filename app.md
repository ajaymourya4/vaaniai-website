**✨ Why Choose Vaani?** Real conversational practice · Instant grammar &
pronunciation feedback · Voice-based learning · Beginner to advanced friendly ·
Supportive and encouraging experience.

## 3. Feature Inventory (from listing + screenshots)

1. **Practice Hub — Topics**: Travel, Work & Career, Food & Cooking,
   Movies & Entertainment, Health & Lifestyle, Daily Life.
2. **Role Play mode**: scenario-based conversations (interviews, travel, etc.).
3. **Voice conversations** with an AI English teacher ("Hold to speak").
4. **Real-time accuracy scoring** (e.g. "93% ACCURACY") on each spoken sentence,
   with mispronounced words highlighted inline.
5. **Words to Practice**: per-word pronunciation drills with IPA
   (e.g. *travel /ˈtrævəl/*), Listen playback, and per-word accuracy % tracking.
6. **Instant correction** of grammar, pronunciation, and word choice mid-conversation.

## 5. Audience & Search Psychology

- **Core user**: understands English (passive skill) but hesitates to speak —
  fear of mistakes, translating in their head, freezing in interviews/meetings.
  Heavily represented in India + South/Southeast Asia ESL markets.
- **Trigger moments**: upcoming job interview, workplace meetings, travel,
  moving abroad, exam speaking sections, general self-improvement.
- **What converts**: pages that first *answer the searcher's actual question*,
  then show that consistent speaking practice is the fix — and that Vaani gives
  a judgment-free partner + instant feedback 24/7. Research consensus: 10–20
  min/day of actual speaking is the single highest-leverage habit.

### 6.2 Long-tail keywords → dedicated guide pages (/guides/*)

High purchase-intent or high-volume questions. One page each; each answers the
question first, then integrates Vaani usage. Each also backs a homepage FAQ
entry with a "Learn More" link.

| # | Long-tail keyword (target query) | Searcher's real question | Guide slug |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | how to speak english fluently | "What do I actually do to become fluent?" | `how-to-speak-english-fluently` |
| 2 | practice english speaking at home / alone / without a partner | "I have nobody to talk to in English" | `practice-english-speaking-at-home` |
| 3 | english speaking practice for job interviews | "Interview soon — how do I not freeze?" | `english-speaking-practice-for-job-interviews` |
| 4 | how to improve english pronunciation | "People don't understand my accent" | `improve-english-pronunciation` |
| 5 | how to speak english confidently without fear/hesitation | "I know English but I hesitate" | `speak-english-confidently-without-fear` |
| 6 | english conversation practice online | "Where can I practice conversation right now?" | `english-conversation-practice-online` |
| 7 | practice english speaking with AI | "Does AI practice actually work? Which app?" | `practice-english-speaking-with-ai` |
| 8 | daily english speaking practice (routine) | "Give me a daily routine I can stick to" | `daily-english-speaking-practice` |

Rationale:
- #2, #5 map exactly to Vaani's core positioning ("understand English but feel
  nervous or stuck") — highest message-match → highest conversion.
- #3 is the classic trigger-moment query (deadline pressure → installs).
- #7 captures the surging "AI speaking app" comparison traffic.
- #1, #6 are the volume plays that feed the rest via internal links.

### 6.4 On-page SEO plan

- **Homepage title**: `Vaani – English Speaking Practice App | Speak English Fluently with AI`
- **Homepage sections**: hero (H1: speak English with confidence) → download →
  screenshots (keyword-rich alt text) → how it works → guides grid → FAQ
  (question-format H3s, each with Learn More → guide) → final CTA → footer
  (guide links = crawl paths + anchor text).
- **Guides**: H1 = target query phrasing; direct answer in first 2 sentences
  (featured-snippet bait); numbered steps (HowTo-ish structure); app
  integration section; mini-FAQ with FAQPage schema; Article + Breadcrumb
  schema; cross-links between related guides; CTA to Play Store.
- **Schema**: SoftwareApplication + Organization sitewide; FAQPage only where
  the FAQ is visible; Article + BreadcrumbList + FAQPage on guides.
- **Assets**: descriptive image filenames + alt text (done); sitemap via
  @astrojs/sitemap; llms.txt updated with guide URLs.
